How to evaluate a used reel mower

Walk-behind greens mowers cut at heights between roughly 0.100" and 0.200" using a precision reel-and-bedknife design. When evaluating used units, reel condition is the most important factor. New reels typically measure around 5" in diameter; they are generally considered serviceable down to about 4.5". A reel with significant life remaining is worth more than one nearing the service limit.

Check for nicks or bends along the reel blades and inspect the bedknife edge. A clean paper-cut test — where the mower cuts a strip of paper cleanly across the full width — is a reliable field indicator of reel-to-bedknife contact quality. Also ask whether the machine has been backlapped or spin-ground recently, and whether the bedknife has been replaced.

Hours matter, but type of use matters too. A mower from a well-maintained private club putting green will wear differently than one from a high-traffic daily-fee course. Ask where the machine was used and how it was maintained.
