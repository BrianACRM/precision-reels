Used walk-behind greens mowers for golf courses and homeowners

Precision Reels specializes in quality used walk-behind greens mowers — the close-cutting reel mowers used on golf course putting greens, short game areas, and residential putting greens. Inventory includes brands such as Jacobsen, Toro, and John Deere, with each listing showing stock number, price, actual photos, and condition details so buyers can make an informed decision before reaching out.

Walk-behind greens mowers are purpose-built machines. They cut at heights measured in fractions of an inch — typically between 0.100" and 0.200" — using a reel and bedknife design that produces a clean, scissor-like cut. Buying used is common in the golf and turf industry because these machines are built for commercial use and often have years of service life remaining when they leave a course fleet.

Buying a used greens mower

Key inspection points include reel diameter remaining, bedknife condition, hours of use, and whether the mower has been recently backlapped or ground. A used greens mower in good working condition will cut a strip of paper cleanly along the full width of the reel. Ask about any known issues, what accessories are included, and whether pickup or freight shipping can be arranged.
