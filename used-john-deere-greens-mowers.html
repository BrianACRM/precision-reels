Buying a used John Deere greens mower

Used John Deere greens mowers enter the market when golf courses upgrade to newer equipment, consolidate their fleet to a single brand, or downsize their maintenance operation. These machines are built for commercial daily use, so a well-maintained used unit often has significant cutting life remaining.

When evaluating a used John Deere greens mower, review the reel and bedknife condition carefully. Ask for photos of the reel face, the bedknife edge, and underneath the cutting unit. Confirm whether the machine cuts paper cleanly across the full width of the reel — this is the standard field check for reel-to-bedknife contact. Also ask what accessories are included: grass catchers, rollers, covers, and transport equipment add practical value to a used purchase.

Parts and service support for John Deere equipment is widely available through the John Deere dealer network, which can be a practical advantage when sourcing replacement components for an older used unit.
