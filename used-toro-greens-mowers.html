About used Toro Greensmaster mowers

The Toro Greensmaster is a walk-behind reel mower designed for the cutting heights required on golf course putting greens — typically between 0.100" and 0.200" height of cut. These machines use a precision reel-and-bedknife system and are engineered for commercial-grade daily use. Used Greensmaster units are widely available because courses regularly rotate fleet equipment, making quality used machines accessible at a fraction of new pricing.

When evaluating a used Toro Greensmaster, the most important factors are reel diameter remaining, bedknife condition, and whether the unit cuts paper cleanly across the full reel width. Ask about hours, recent grinding or backlapping, and what is included — accessories like grass catchers, rollers, and transport covers add value to a used purchase.
