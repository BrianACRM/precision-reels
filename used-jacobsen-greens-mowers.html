About Jacobsen greens mowers

Jacobsen greens mowers use a reel-and-bedknife cutting system designed for the low cutting heights required on putting greens. The Eclipse series is known for its lightweight frame and precise height-of-cut adjustments, making it a popular choice for courses that want a quality cut without heavy roller imprint. PGM models offer a more traditional configuration and are widely available on the used market as courses cycle their fleets.

When buying a used Jacobsen greens mower, the reel and bedknife condition are the primary factors to evaluate. Ask about reel diameter remaining, whether the bedknife has been replaced, and how recently the unit was serviced. A machine with documented service history from a well-run course will generally carry more confidence than one with unknown records.
